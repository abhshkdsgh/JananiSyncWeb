# JananiSync — Release notes

## Delivery outcome and baby details

Record delivery and newborn details alongside the Labour Care Guide (LCG) and Partograph:

- Delivery method and date/time, with a separate indication field for LSCS.
- Baby’s sex, birth weight, live birth or stillbirth, and Apgar scores at 1 and 5 minutes.
- Additional notes for resuscitation, NICU care, and details of each baby in multiple births.

These details are available in entry history and included in chart exports.

## Easier chart viewing

- A highlighted **Chart zoom** panel above the Partograph makes **Zoom in**, **Zoom out**, and **Reset to 100%** easier to find. It also shows the current zoom percentage.
- On a computer, hold **Ctrl** or **Cmd** and scroll the mouse wheel to enlarge or reduce the whole app. Press **Ctrl+0** or **Cmd+0** to reset. This zoom resets when the app reloads.
- Two-finger pinch zoom remains available on the Partograph.

## Clearer printing and PDF export

- **Export PDF / Print** now sits directly below the LCG or Partograph heading.
- Contraction dots, diagonal stripes, and solid shading have been improved for both half-hour sections in exported Partographs.
- Half-hour readings that cross a 12-hour page boundary now appear on the next export page, including graph readings and text entries.

## Clearer sign-in and navigation

- Sign-in now uses **Username**. Use the email address entered when signing up.
- Sign-in and sign-up explain that your JananiSync password does not need to match your email provider’s password.
- The woman-list button is now labelled **Open LCG**.
- **Admin console** and **Patient dashboard** have leading icons in both desktop and compact menus.
- Info icons explain that **access level means seniority within the organisation**: higher numbers indicate greater seniority. This explanation also appears beside fields used to assign seniority.

## For organisation administrators

The organisation access code is prominently displayed at the top of the Admin dashboard. Select the text or use the copy icon to share it with staff requesting to join.

## Privacy policy

Privacy Policy **version 1.3** updates operator and privacy-contact details. The acceptance process has been corrected to match the updated policy. Users will be asked to review and accept the current version before continuing to organisation and clinical records.

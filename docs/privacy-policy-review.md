## Legal basis checked

This is an India-oriented drafting framework. Recheck the law and notifications on the actual publication date, including amendments/corrigenda and sector-specific medical-record duties.

- The [Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, official Gazette text reproduced by WIPO](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/15063) address sensitive health/password information, public privacy policies, collection and use, review/correction, withdrawal, retention, sharing/transfers, security, and a named grievance officer with redress within one month. Applicability and the operator/processor context need legal review. The [Government's 2011 clarification](https://www.pib.gov.in/newsite/erelcontent.aspx?lang=2&reg=48&relid=74990) distinguishes certain contracted processing obligations; it does not remove the Rule 4 policy requirement.
- The [DPDP Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) provides the future applicable framework for purposes, consent, safeguards, rights, and represented persons. The draft qualifies statutory rights by applicability and commencement.
- The [official commencement notification, G.S.R. 843(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) phases commencement: specified institutional provisions on publication, specified consent-manager provisions after one year, and most substantive processing/rights provisions plus section 44(2) after eighteen months. The draft is dated before that eighteen-month stage. Do not describe all DPDP duties as already commenced, or treat transition as permission to ignore existing law.
- The [DPDP Rules, 2025, G.S.R. 846(E)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf) also commence in phases. Their operational requirements need implementation, not simply policy wording. In particular, plan an independent intelligible notice, specific purposes/data descriptions, usable withdrawal/rights links, safeguards, and breach procedures before the relevant rules apply. Verify the then-current official regulator complaint channel rather than inventing a URL.

## Versioned policy and decision evidence

`docs/privacy-policy.md` is the source for `PrivacyPolicyDocument.CONTENT`, the exact UTF-8 SHA-256, and the archived `docs/privacy/1.0.md` snapshot. `docs/privacy/1.0-manifest.json` also records the effective date, presentation version, button wording, and both decision statements. Generate with `python3 scripts/generate_privacy_policy.py`; validate without writing using `--check`. Published snapshots must remain unchanged. Bump the policy version and regenerate for changes that affect the recorded text; retain the old archive alongside historical decisions.

The dialog allows reading without a decision before signup. Submitting signup creates a provisional Firebase account so the subsequent explicit acceptance or denial can be linked to its UID. Clinical/organization access must remain blocked until server confirmation of current-policy acceptance. Denial history is retained and access stays blocked. This must be made clear on the signup form before account creation, rather than creating an account as a side effect of pressing Deny.

Decision history is account-action evidence. Server timestamps, authentication binding, append-only client rules, and policy hashes help assess integrity. A client-supplied time, user-agent, locale, or action source is not independently verified. An unverified email is not proof of its owner. Even verified-account actions are not proof that every word was read, patient consent was obtained, or a record is legally conclusive. Privileged project administrators can bypass Firestore client rules; deployment access controls, protected exports/backups, and procedures to preserve records and chain of custody remain operational responsibilities.
